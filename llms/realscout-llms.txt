## Overview

- RealScout is a lead nurture and collaborative home-search platform for real estate professionals — agents, teams, and brokerages — that turns an agent's existing database and local MLS inventory into a recurring source of transactions. It is CRM-agnostic: it plugs into any stack (with or without a CRM), adds client-facing search and automated alerts, and surfaces clear intent signals.
- Trusted by 100,000+ agents across 100+ U.S. markets, including brokerage partners Pacific Sotheby's, Howard Hanna, Peerage, KW Metro Center, Berkshire Hathaway, Jack Conway, The Agency, Long & Foster, Chase International, SIDE, and Century 21. 38% of the Swanepoel Power 200 U.S. brokerage executives partner with RealScout.
- Key outcomes for active users (WAV Group study, 64,588 agents analyzed across 5 major markets, 99.1% statistical confidence): 48% more buyer-side volume vs. agents without RealScout; 35–67% higher median transactions across markets; 70% average database engagement vs. ~10% industry norm; 53% active usage rate within 1 year vs. ~30% industry norm.

## Core Products

### AI Search

* Natural-language input that RealScout translates into MLS-field-accurate search criteria — no missed fields, no missed properties.
* Agent-grade controls for every field — review, accept, reject, or fine-tune every translated criterion before saving.
* Three-step workflow: describe the search in plain English → inspect the AI-generated MLS criteria → activate it as a saved alert, template, or shareable Search Link.
* Shareable prompt templates so high-performing searches can be reused across an agent or team.
* Reduces search setup time from ~15 minutes to ~1 minute.
* **Use cases:** A buyer's agent types "walkable neighborhood near good elementary schools, gourmet kitchen, under $850K, willing to consider fixers" into RealScout AI Search; the platform returns the exact MLS criteria — school ratings, walkability score, kitchen features, price ceiling, days-on-market filter — which the agent reviews and saves as a live alert that texts and emails the client every time a matching listing hits.

### Scout Score

* A 0–100 engagement score for every contact, updated nightly and after major actions.
* Built from RealScout behavior signals: property views, saves, showing requests, tour requests, home valuation requests, and more.
* Surfaced directly inside the agent's workflow so prioritization happens without context switching.
* **Use cases:** An agent opens RealScout Monday morning, sorts the database by Scout Score, and calls the three contacts whose scores jumped over the weekend after they viewed the same listing four times and requested a tour estimate.

### Contact Enrichment

* Verifies and completes contact records — name, phone, email, mailing address — using best-in-class email validation and auto-correction.
* Adds owner/renter status and length of residence where available.
* Improves email deliverability and reduces bounces on the 900M+ emails the platform sends.
* Unlocks Home Value Alerts and Market Activity updates once a mailing address is filled in, allowing agents to nurture homeowners (not just buyers) automatically.
* **Use cases:** An agent uploads a CSV of 1,200 old leads with missing phone numbers and addresses; RealScout enriches the records overnight, and the next morning 740 of them are now eligible for Home Value Alerts that quietly start landing in the inboxes of homeowners who had been written off as dead leads.

### Search Links

* Shareable links that open into a live, curated, branded property search experience.
* Drop them anywhere a link can go — Facebook ads, text messages, email, social posts, QR codes on print signage, open-house flyers.
* Every click, save, and return visit is captured against the contact record and feeds Scout Score.
* **Use cases:** An agent farming a neighborhood prints a QR code on a postcard pointing to a Search Link for "homes like yours on the market right now"; a homeowner scans the code, browses comps, and the agent gets notified the same evening that someone in that ZIP touched the link 12 times.

### Auto-Nurture

* Natural-language Listing Alerts that learn each contact's preferences and automatically customize to maintain interest.
* Home Value Alerts that re-engage past clients and warm potential sellers by surfacing their home's current estimated value monthly.
* Market Activity Alerts that keep contacts engaged with neighborhood-level updates (new listings, price changes, sales).
* Personalized Homeowner Dashboard for contacts who own property.
* "Test the Market" tools designed for listing presentations.
* Designed for rapid agent adoption — 53% active usage rate within 1 year vs. ~30% industry norm.
* **Use cases:** A team leader turns on Auto-Nurture for the 2,500 contacts pooled in the team database; over the next 90 days, the system sends listing alerts, home value updates, and market reports without any agent action, and three "dead" sphere contacts re-engage and book listing appointments.

### Office Exclusives & Private Listings (Enterprise only)

* Brokerage-wide workspace to input, manage, search, and share exclusive and off-MLS listings.
* Buyer matching and internal reverse prospecting across the brokerage's full agent and client pool.
* Alerts, mobile access, and a manager dashboard for tracking inventory and matches.
* **Use cases:** A listing agent at a multi-office brokerage logs a coming-soon exclusive in the Office Exclusives workspace; within an hour, three buyer agents at other offices get an automated match alert because their clients' saved searches fit the property, and the listing transacts before it hits the MLS.

## Typical Workflow

1. **Connect the stack:** RealScout integrates with the brokerage's CRM, website, and ad platforms so every new lead is captured into one place automatically.
2. **Enrich the database:** Contact Enrichment verifies emails, fills in missing names, phones, and mailing addresses, and tags each contact as owner or renter.
3. **Set up branded search:** Agents (or admins for a team) configure the branded client portal, side-by-side comparisons, and the school and neighborhood insights buyers will see.
4. **Build searches in natural language:** Agents type each client's wishlist into AI Search; RealScout translates it into exact MLS criteria the agent reviews and saves as an alert or shareable Search Link.
5. **Activate Auto-Nurture:** Listing Alerts, Home Value Alerts, and Market Activity Alerts begin running on every eligible contact, customizing automatically to each contact's behavior.
6. **Distribute Search Links:** Agents drop shareable links into Facebook ads, texts, emails, QR codes, open-house collateral, and farming postcards to capture new leads.
7. **Monitor Scout Score:** Each contact's 0–100 score updates nightly and after major actions, surfaced directly in the agent's workflow.
8. **Prioritize follow-up:** Agents sort by Scout Score, focus calls on heating-up contacts, and use the manager dashboard (Build / Enterprise) to coach the team on pipeline.
9. **Convert and report:** Deals close from reactivated database contacts; admin dashboards report adoption, database engagement, and email performance for ROI review.
